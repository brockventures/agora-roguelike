04 Map & fleet
MapNode
Flat disc, 3px ink contour, solid ink drop (4,4), shadow cut on a chord at 0.3r, lower right. Same geometry as HudTheme.draw_flat_disc. Docked or focused nodes get a dashed ochre ring that marches in 4 steps.
HullRow · StepTrack
Dispatch is map-driven: pick hull → pick station on the map → confirm. Status color follows state (docked teal, transit ochre, risk rust) and the status text always says it in words.
