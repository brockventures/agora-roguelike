06 Art direction
card art · 4:3
Do: heavy ligne-claire contour, 2–3 flat value steps, mineral/rust palette, one figure or object, readable at 200px wide.
✕
Don't: text, numbers, signage, logos, signatures or watermarks inside art. No airbrush, bloom, lens flare or soft gradients.
Where art lives: map nodes, lane markers, card viewports, landing hero. Never a static full-screen backdrop behind the HUD.
